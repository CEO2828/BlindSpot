What this view can — and cannot — tell you

Connected allocation adds the value of each held company with a reviewed supplier relationship, counting that company once per supplier. It is not a prediction of losses or a measure of revenue at risk. Supplier groups can overlap. Coverage means reviewed within the indexed excerpts, not across every public source. The included source records were checked with AI assistance; human confirmation is recommended. Fresh Gemini extractions require review before they appear here.
