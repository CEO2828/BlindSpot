How to read this research preview

Each holding counts once per supplier. Groups can overlap; do not add their percentages. Unassessed value stays in the denominator. Evidence coverage is limited to indexed excerpts, not every public source. Existing source-checked records were reviewed with AI assistance; human confirmation is recommended. No live prices, brokerage access, or investment forecasts.
