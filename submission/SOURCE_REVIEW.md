# Source inspection — September 26, 2026

AI-assisted inspection of live SEC text reconfirmed the three manufacturing relationships. This is not human sign-off, model evaluation, or a new source-byte hash comparison.

- [NVIDIA FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm): manufacturing section names TSMC and Samsung as wafer foundries.
- [AMD FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000018/amd-20251227.htm): wafer foundry section names TSMC for HPC, FPGA, and adaptive SoC products; other arrangements are also disclosed.
- [Broadcom FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016825000121/avgo-20251102.htm): approximately 95% of wafers manufactured by its contract manufacturers were produced by TSMC in FY2025. This percentage does not scale portfolio amounts.

Immutable snapshots and reviewer attribution are preserved. No human sign-off or provider execution was fabricated.
